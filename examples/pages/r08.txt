This recipe has been tested dozens of times in our test kitchen and adapted for high altitude baking as well. For best results choose bananas that are heavily speckled with brown spots as the starch has fully converted to sugar which gives the bread its natural sweetness without adding extra refined sugar to the batter. Room temperature ingredients blend more evenly so pull your butter and eggs from the refrigerator about thirty minutes before you begin mixing. The secret to a moist crumb is avoiding overmixing once the flour goes in; stir just until the streaks disappear. Line your loaf pan with parchment paper leaving an overhang on the sides for easy removal. Bake on the middle rack and rotate halfway through for even browning. Let it cool in the pan for ten minutes then transfer to a wire rack. Store wrapped at room temperature for up to three days or freeze individual slices for quick breakfasts. Some readers add a cream cheese swirl or a streusel topping for extra decadence while others swap in walnuts or pecans for crunch. Either way the chocolate chips melt into little pockets throughout the loaf.
